01

Recolored to sit closer to the soft, storybook-green reference: warm cream and white still hold the structural weight (headers, surfaces), while matcha green, butter yellow, sky blue, and cocoa brown handle accents, tag strips, and highlights.

02

typography

Baloo 2 stays on headings after all — dialed back to weight 600–700 (not the moodboard's 800) and used at more restrained sizes, so its rounded warmth still comes through without tipping back into playful. Everything else about the professional shift — photography, tighter corners, hairline borders — holds.

03

corner radius scale — the big change

This is the main structural shift. Everything moves down one or two steps: cards go from 20–36px down to 14px, inputs to 10px, and small controls to 8px. Only pills (buttons, filter chips, tags) stay fully rounded — that contrast is what keeps it from feeling stiff.

04

recipe card anatomy

The biggest content change: real photography replaces the illustrated color-block covers. A colored tag strip sits directly under the photo — the color signals the primary dietary category at a glance, before you read a single word.

05

search & filter components

Same pill-shaped filter chips as before, tightened up: solid moss fill for the active state, thin hairline borders instead of thick 2px outlines, no dashed borders anywhere — those read as scrapbook, not toolbar.

06

do / don't

✓ lean into
  • Real, warm, appetizing food photography
  • Colored tag strips that encode category at a glance
  • Hairline 1px borders over thick 2px outlines
  • Pill-shaped controls against otherwise square-cornered cards
✕ avoid
  • Hand-drawn character mascots or emoji covers
  • Dashed / dotted borders
  • Card radius above 16px
  • Tilted or "collaged" card rotation
